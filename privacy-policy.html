Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

⚠️ Portfolio Disclosure: This website is a portfolio/demonstration project. No real e-commerce transactions are processed. Contact form submissions on this demo site do not transmit data to any server.

1. Who We Are

CREAM IT is an artisan bakery brand. This website (https://amit100baidya.github.io/CreamIt/) is a demonstration/portfolio website. References to "we", "us", or "our" refer to the CREAM IT brand.

2. What Information We Collect

When you visit this website, we may collect:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, browser type, device type, and general geographic region — collected automatically via Google Analytics 4.
  • Contact form data: If you submit the contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message. Note: On this demo site, form submissions are not sent to any server.
  • Newsletter signups: Email addresses entered in the newsletter form. Note: On this demo site, no emails are stored or transmitted.
  • Local storage data: Cart contents, wishlist items, and theme preferences are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

3. Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-KYCSY62Y8E) to understand how visitors interact with our content. GA4 collects:

  • Pages viewed and navigation patterns
  • Time on site and bounce rate
  • Device and browser information
  • General geographic location (country/city level)
  • Event interactions (button clicks, product views, cart additions)

We use GA4 in basic analytics mode. IP addresses are anonymized. We do not use GA4 for advertising targeting on this site.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Cookies

This website uses cookies set by Google Analytics for analytics purposes. These cookies help us understand website usage patterns. The main cookies are:

  • _ga — Distinguishes users. Expires in 2 years.
  • _ga_* — Stores session state. Expires in 2 years.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect your experience on this site.

5. How We Use Information

  • To understand how visitors use our website and improve the experience
  • To respond to contact form submissions (when operating as a live bakery)
  • To send newsletters (when you subscribe and we have an email service)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Retention

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months by default. Local storage data (cart, wishlist) remains in your browser until you clear it manually.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking

To exercise these rights, contact us via the Contact page.

8. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites (social media, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page.
