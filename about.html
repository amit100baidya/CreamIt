Our Story

Where passion meets perfection in every bite

Baked With Love Since 2020

Founded in 2020, Cream It Bakery has been dedicated to creating delicious, high-quality baked goods that bring joy to our community. What started as a small family passion project in a tiny kitchen has grown into a beloved local institution.

We believe that great baking is about more than just ingredients — it's about the love, care, and attention to detail that goes into every loaf, cake, and pastry we create. Our bakers arrive before dawn each day to ensure everything is fresh and ready for you.

From birthday cakes to wedding masterpieces, from your morning croissant to a cozy loaf of sourdough — we pour our hearts into every single thing we bake.
