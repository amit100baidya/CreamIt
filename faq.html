🎂 Custom Orders & Customization

We recommend placing custom cake orders at least 48–72 hours in advance for standard designs. For elaborate multi-tier wedding or event cakes, please allow 5–7 days. Urgent same-day orders may be accommodated for simpler designs — contact us to check availability.

Absolutely! Every cake from CREAM IT can be personalized. You can choose your flavor, frosting, toppings, and add a personal message written in icing. For fully custom designs (themed cakes, character cakes, photo prints), please describe your vision via our contact form or use the Cake Builder on our website as a starting point.

Yes! We offer edible photo printing on cakes. Please share a high-resolution image (min. 300 DPI) when placing your order. Photo print cakes start from ₹999 for 1kg and require at least 48 hours advance notice. Note: We can only print images you have the right to use.

🌿 Dietary & Ingredients

Yes! We have a dedicated range of 100% eggless cakes and pastries. Our eggless options use plant-based substitutes that deliver the same moist, delicious texture. Look for the "Eggless" tag on our menu, or use the Eggless filter. Popular eggless options include our Pineapple Cake, Mango Mousse Cake, and Festival Special Cake.

We strive to use natural food-grade colors wherever possible and no artificial preservatives in our freshly baked goods. Our products are best consumed within 2–3 days of purchase. Some specialty decorations (fondant, edible glitter) may contain permitted food-grade additives — please ask if you have specific concerns.

Please always inform us of any allergies when placing your order. While we take every precaution, our kitchen handles common allergens including gluten, dairy, eggs, nuts, and soy. We cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment. For severe allergies, please consult with us directly before ordering.

🚚 Delivery & Pickup

Same-day delivery is available for select items (pastries, cookies, cupcakes, breads) if ordered before 12:00 PM. Custom cakes require advance notice. Delivery availability depends on your location — please contact us to confirm. Our delivery time estimates on the menu indicate approximate preparation time, not door-to-door delivery time.

All cakes are packed in food-grade boxes with internal supports to prevent movement. Tiered and tall cakes have additional stabilization. We recommend storing cakes in a cool place (below 22°C) and consuming within 24 hours of delivery. Do not place cakes in direct sunlight or near heat sources during transport.

Once your order is confirmed, we'll keep you updated via WhatsApp or email at key stages — order confirmed → baking → out for delivery. For time-sensitive orders, don't hesitate to call or message us directly. Full order tracking integration is coming soon!

💳 Payment & Pricing

We accept a wide range of payment methods including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Debit/Credit Cards, and Cash on Delivery for eligible orders. A 50% advance is required for custom orders above ₹2,000. Full payment is required before dispatch.

The prices shown on our website are indicative demo prices for a standard size/option. Final pricing may vary based on your chosen size, customization complexity, and seasonal ingredient availability. We will always confirm the final price before accepting payment for custom orders. All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹).

Yes! We offer bulk/corporate pricing for orders of 20+ items or large event orders. Contact us with your event details and we'll provide a custom quote. We cater to weddings, corporate events, birthday parties, festivals, and more.
